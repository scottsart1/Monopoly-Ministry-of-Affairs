# Velvet & Roulette

A private spin-the-wheel game for two, in a single `index.html` — no build, no server, no dependencies beyond Google Fonts.

**How it works**

1. Open `index.html` (or the published page) on a phone or laptop.
2. Read the house rules together and tap in.
3. Spin — tap for a normal spin, or hold the hub to charge a wilder one. The wheel picks the activity and rolls a secret timer for it.
4. Start the clock. When the bell rings, everything stops and you spin again.
5. Either player can veto any spin — no reason needed.
6. There are no points. The only winner is whoever finishes.

- **Plot twists** — some rounds get interrupted mid-clock with a surprise instruction.
- **Double or nothing** — when the bell rings, the game sometimes offers a coin flip: win 60 seconds of overtime, or lose and serve 60 seconds of talk-only.
- **Focus rolls** — massage, ice and blindfold rounds pick a body zone; toy rounds pick from your (editable) drawer.
- **Night Report** — rounds survived, time under the bell, peak heat, twists, jackpots, vetoes and coin flips, plus an aftercare card.

**Skip the timers**

A menu toggle removes the bell everywhere: rounds show the activity with a *Done* button, scene steps let you move on when you're ready, and the coin flip is skipped. Rounds and heat still count.

**Backup & transfer**

Menu → *Backup & transfer* gives you a backup code (sheet, plan, menu, names, stats — encoded, not encrypted) to copy and paste on any device, plus *Save as file* where the page can offer one. *Restore* accepts a pasted code or a backup file and replaces everything on that device after a second tap.

**Escalation Ladder**

A guided climb instead of random spins. Tap **🪜 Escalation ladder**: it presents an activity at the current rung, and after each one you choose **Escalate ↑**, **Stay →** or **De-escalate ↓**. The rungs run Warming → Wandering → Wanting → Reckless → The edge; the top rung draws on the hottest, "maybe"-gated items in your overlap, so climbing all the way reaches the edges of what you've both agreed to. Everything is still sheet-gated, timed by the bell (or free, with Skip the timers), and drops off for the night like any other round.

**No repeats tonight**

On by default (toggle in the menu): once you play a round it leaves the wheel for the rest of the evening, so you keep getting new things. When the wheel runs dry it brings them all back automatically; you can also bring them back any time from the menu, and a fresh night (Rematch) resets everything.

**Customizing (Menu)**

Pace (Quickie / Classic / Marathon), starting heat, chaos dials (specials, twists, plot twists, coin flips, hidden clock, sound), your names, the toy drawer, and the activity list itself — untick, add your own with a heat level and time range, or reset to the house menu. Everything is saved in the browser's local storage on that device only; nothing leaves the phone.
